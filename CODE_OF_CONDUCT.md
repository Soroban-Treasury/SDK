# Code of Conduct

We want this project to be a respectful place to work for everyone.

## Our standard

- Be kind. Assume good intent and ask before you assume otherwise.
- Be specific and constructive when you give feedback. Criticize the work, not the person.
- Welcome people of every background and experience level.
- Respect other people's time: keep issues and pull requests focused.

Not acceptable: harassment, insults, personal attacks, discrimination, publishing other people's private information, or anything else that would be out of place in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues and pull requests that break this code, and may ban people who break it repeatedly or seriously. To report a problem, contact a maintainer privately, for example through a private security advisory on this repository.

This code applies in the project's repositories and in public spaces when someone represents the project.
